abstract: >-
  Proposed AEFIN, a novel framework for non-stationary time series forecasting that combines Fourier Transform-based decomposition to separate stationary and non-stationary components, cross-attention mechanisms for enhanced information sharing, and Fourier analysis for capturing periodic patterns. Designed a custom loss function that integrates MSE and MAE across time- and frequency-domain components to improve model robustness. The proposed framework achieved SOTA performance on most benchmark datasets, consistently improving MSE and MAE when integrated with mainstream Transformer- and CNN-based forecasting models.
