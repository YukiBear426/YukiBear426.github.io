abstract: >-
  Developed AEFIN, a deep learning framework for non-stationary time series forecasting, combining Fourier analysis networks, deep neural networks, and a cross-attention mechanism. Designed a novel loss function with time-domain and frequency-domain constraints to improve accuracy and robustness. Achieved superior performance compared to common models in mean squared error and mean absolute error, demonstrating strong forecasting capabilities for financial, weather, and traffic data.
